About CardioGuard Predictive Engine

Technical documentation, classifier metrics, and model feature weights for the health risk classifier.

Machine Learning Framework

The core classification is powered by a Random Forest Classifier trained on a synthetic clinical cohort containing detailed diagnostic information. The model aggregates scores from a forest of 100 decision trees to estimate whether a patient falls into a Low, Medium, or High Risk category.

Features are split dynamically: variables such as Age, BMI, Diet Quality, Sleep Hours, Stress Level, and Lifestyle choices are processed directly through the estimator to output the risk classification. Additional clinical vitals (Blood Pressure, Heart Rate, Glucose, Cholesterol, Symptoms) are utilized downstream by a Rule-Based Clinical Engine to generate personalized diagnostic advice, warnings, and DASH diet regimens.

Key Classifier Architecture:

  • Feature Preprocessing: Continuous variables scaled using StandardScaler; categorical inputs mapped using LabelEncoder.
  • Ensemble Architecture: 100 Decision Trees with Gini Impurity metric criteria.
  • Out-of-Bag Validation: Random partitions utilized for evaluation metrics.
